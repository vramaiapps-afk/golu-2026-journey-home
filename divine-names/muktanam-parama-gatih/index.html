JH-2.03 · Chapter 2: The Compassionate Lord

Name 12 of 54

मुक्तानां परमा गतिः முக்தானாம் பரமா கதி: Muktānāṃ Paramā Gatiḥ

The One whom liberated souls consider their highest goal — eternal service to Him.

Acharya's Teaching

Lakṣmaṇa's own words give this name its meaning: liberated souls (muktās) regard eternal kainkāryam to the Lord in Vaikuṇṭha as their supreme goal (paramā gatiḥ) — and Lakṣmaṇa realized he had the rarer chance to begin that very service while still living. What liberated souls wait for after death, he refused to postpone.

The Story

Dr. Venkatesh tells of Rāma, departing for exile, refusing to let Sītā accompany him onto thorned forest paths. Sītā poses a test question first: define heaven and hell. When Rāma gives the conventional answer — Indra's world and Yama's world — Sītā tells him he's entirely wrong: heaven and hell mean something different to everyone. To her, she says simply, "Being with you is heaven; being without you is hell." Rāma relents and takes her.

Meanwhile, Lakṣmaṇa, who had waited outside, now performs śaraṇāgati at Rāma's feet, begging to serve him in exile. When Rāma questions why he must come — the exile was Kaikeyi's curse on Rāma alone — Lakṣmaṇa explains: liberated souls in Vaikuṇṭha long for the chance to serve Rāma eternally after death, but he has the rarer fortune of serving Him right now, while still alive on earth — and he refuses to let that chance slip by. Rāma takes both Sītā and Lakṣmaṇa with him.

Living This Divine Name

The highest goal isn't a distant reward reserved for later — it's the chance to serve Him today.

Today's Reflection & Practice

Find one small way to serve someone today, as if it were your highest goal.

Chant

Om Muktānāṃ Paramāya Gataye Namaḥa

Connection to the Journey Home

This name names the very destination of the Journey Home itself — not a place, but an eternal, joyful service at His feet, which every liberated soul seeks above all else.
