JH-5.08 · Chapter 5: The Bestower of Grace

Name 44 of 54

विधाता விதாதா Vidhātā

The One who creates in a special way and arranges each soul's fruits of karma.

Acharya's Teaching

Dhātā creates the world in general from its source material; Vidhātā goes beyond the usual laws to arrange each soul's creation and its fruits according to its own past karma. That is why He is also called the One who fixes destiny (vidhi).

The Story

Dr. Venkatesh takes up the question left last time: if Dhātā and Vidhātā both mean creator, what is the difference? Vi means special, so Vidhātā is the One who creates in a special way. He turns to the Mahābhārata. When Yudhiṣṭhira was born to Kuntī before Gandhārī had borne a child, Gandhārī, consumed with jealousy, struck her own womb. What came out was a mass of flesh. Vyāsa divided it into one hundred and one pieces and placed them in jars of ghee, and in time the hundred Kauravas and the daughter Duḥśalā were born.

By ordinary law a child comes only from a mother's womb. So who arranged life, body and karma in those jars? Dr. Venkatesh says it was not an ordinary act of Brahmā, but a special creation by the Lord who is within him. Even though Vyāsa performed it, Nārāyaṇa stood within Vyāsa and did it.

Living This Divine Name

What looks like fate, even the harshest, is held in hands that arrange it with care. We are never just the leftover of chance.

Today's Reflection & Practice

Think of one hard circumstance of your life and pray for the grace to see it as arranged, not accidental.

Chant

Om Vidhātrē Namaḥa

Connection to the Journey Home

The same One who arranges each soul's destiny is the One who arranges its way home, bending even karma toward the end of the path.
