The Film — Sūḻ Visumbu, imagined

A short film that visualises the journey described in Nammāḻvār's Sūḻ Visumbu (Thiruvāymoṛi 10.9): the soul led along the path of light to Vaikuṇṭha, where the Lord and Thāyār wait to receive it. We made it for this year's Golu using AI video tools.

The images are our imagination, made with AI. They are not a depiction fixed by tradition. The meaning of each verse is on the Journey Home pages, taken from the discourses of Dr. Venkatesh Swamin. Not loading? Watch on YouTube.
