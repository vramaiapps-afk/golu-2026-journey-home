JH-5.02 · Chapter 5: The Bestower of Grace

Name 38 of 54

शम्भुः ஶம்புஃ Śambhuḥ

The One who bestows true, lasting spiritual joy.

Acharya's Teaching

Śam is true spiritual joy, and Śambhuḥ is the One who gives it. Dr. Venkatesh teaches that Dāsar did not give up love; in one glance he saw where real joy lives. The delight found in family, friends and the world does not hold it. It is found in Perumāḷ alone. He cites Maṇavāḷa Māmuṉi's verse on how He draws His devotees close.

The Story

Dr. Venkatesh opens by noting that ordinary happiness and true peace are different. He recalls Kamal Haasan asking Rajinikanth why he keeps going to the Himalayas, and Rajinikanth answering that there is happiness here, but not peace, so he goes in search of peace. The word śam names that real spiritual joy.

He then tells of Piḷḷai Uraṅkāvilāḍi Dāsar of Srirangam, a bodyguard to the Chola king, so devoted to his wife that he walked behind her holding an umbrella against the sun. Rāmānuja asked why. He said he was captivated by her eyes. Rāmānuja asked: if I showed you more beautiful eyes, what would you do? Dāsar said he would be their slave from that day. Rāmānuja led him before Raṅganatha and prayed that the Lord reveal the eye-beauty He had shown the Āḷvārs. The moment Periyaperumāḷ did so, Dāsar became a servant of Raṅganatha and Rāmānuja, and his wife Ponṇācciyār became Rāmānuja's disciple.

Living This Divine Name

Joys of the world are real, but they are small windows. This name points to the light they were all opening onto.

Today's Reflection & Practice

Ask yourself today which of your joys leave you at peace, and spend a little more time there.

Chant

Om Śambhavē Namaḥa

Connection to the Journey Home

Dāsar's change happened in a single glance. On the soul's journey home, one true sight of the Lord is enough to turn every other attachment into service.
