JH-1.06 · Chapter 1: The Supreme Lord

Name 6 of 54

भूतभृत् பூதப்ருத் Bhūtabhṛt

The One who feeds and sustains every being He has created.

Acharya's Teaching

Dr. Venkatesh draws a direct line from the previous name: Bhūtakṛt (name 5) is the One who creates every being; Bhūtabhṛt (this name) is the One who continues to sustain and nourish what He created. Bhṛt means to bear, feed, or sustain. A poor devotee who knew only this name recited it daily, as Rāmānuja instructed — and prasādam began mysteriously reaching him each day, delivered by Lord Raṅganātha Himself in disguise, proving the name's promise in the most literal way.

The Story

Dr. Venkatesh tells of a poor devotee in Srirangam who, with a large family to feed, kept asking for extra prasādam without offering any recitation in return — until temple staff turned him away. Rāmānuja discovered he knew only the first six names of the Sahasranāma, and told him simply to recite Bhūtabhṛte Namaḥa daily — food would find him.

The man stopped coming to the temple, yet a portion of prasādam began mysteriously disappearing each day. Investigation revealed it was being delivered to him by a servant introducing himself as "Raṅganāthan" — Rāmānuja's disciple. Rāmānuja realized Lord Raṅganātha Himself had taken that form, keeping His word to the humble devotee.

Living This Divine Name

Even the smallest devotion, offered sincerely, is enough for the Lord to provide for what we truly need.

Today's Reflection & Practice

Recite this name once today, trusting that what you need will find you.

Chant

Om Bhūtabhṛte Namaḥa

Connection to the Journey Home

Just as He sustained that devotee daily, He sustains the soul through every stage of the Archirādhi Mārga.
