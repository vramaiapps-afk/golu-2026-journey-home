JH-2.07 · Chapter 2: The Compassionate Lord

Name 16 of 54

क्षेत्रज्ञः க்ஷேத்ரஜ்ஞஃ Kṣetrajñaḥ

The One who chooses the exact right place to grant complete grace.

Acharya's Teaching

Kṣetram means place; Kṣetrajñaḥ means the One who knows the right place. Dr. Venkatesh connects this directly to Thiruvaḷḷuvar's teaching that no undertaking should begin without first understanding the right setting for it (idam aṛithal). Perumāḷ chose Srirangam for daily worship and Thirukkaṇṇapuram specifically for revealing His walk — because only the second location would complete Vibhīṣaṇa's grace with the fullest possible meaning.

The Story

Dr. Venkatesh tells of Vibhīṣaṇa, gifted the deity Raṅganātha by Rāma after the coronation, and granted the honor of nightly worship at Srirangam, where Perumāḷ chose to remain reclining forever. One night, Vibhīṣaṇa asks whether lying in the same pose eternally isn't tiresome, and whether Perumāḷ might walk instead. Raṅganātha agrees — but only at Thirukkaṇṇapuram, on the east coast, on the next Amāvāsya, not at Srirangam.

Puzzled why the two must be separate, Vibhīṣaṇa goes anyway. At Thirukkaṇṇapuram, Saurirāja Perumāḷ walks for him, and Vibhīṣaṇa is overcome — trembling, weeping, momentarily fainting. When he recovers, he realizes: seeing Perumāḷ walk on this particular coast recalled the exact moment of his own surrender to Rāma at Thiruppuḷḷāṇi's shore, where Rāma had walked toward him declaring refuge to all who surrender even once. Had Perumāḷ walked at Srirangam instead, that specific memory would never have surfaced — only this exact place could complete the experience.

Living This Divine Name

Grace often arrives in a specific place or moment for a reason we don't yet see — the setting is never incidental to Him.

Today's Reflection & Practice

Notice today where you feel most drawn to Him, and honor that place.

Chant

Om Kṣetrajñāya Namaḥa

Connection to the Journey Home

Just as He chose Thirukkaṇṇapuram with precision for Vibhīṣaṇa, He has chosen Paramapadam as the exact right destination for the soul's journey home.
